⚠️ About the free pool — straight talk

The free pool is the fastest way to start: zero config, ProxyPilot finds a live proxy for you. But public free proxies are untrusted by definition — anyone can run one.

Don't sign in to Google, banking, or any important account while routed through a free proxy — that sign-in will likely be flagged. For anything sensitive, bring your own proxy. The extension shows a clear warning banner when the free pool is active with an AI service.

FAQ

Good questions

Is this a VPN?
No. A VPN tunnels your whole browser through one server. ProxyPilot routes only the domains you enable through a proxy; everything else connects directly — so it's faster and your other sites keep seeing your real location.
Do I need an account?
No signup, no account, no telemetry. All settings (proxy config, enabled services, custom domains) are stored locally in your browser and never leave your device.
Is the free proxy pool safe?
It's convenient but the proxies are public and untrusted. Don't sign in to important accounts while routed through them. For sensitive use, bring your own proxy — ProxyPilot supports HTTP/HTTPS/SOCKS4/SOCKS5. The app warns you in the UI.
How is it different from a proxy switcher like SwitchyOmega?
40+ ready-made services in one click, automatic protocol detection, proxy testing, auto-rotation when a proxy dies, and a smart free pool that finds a fast working proxy for you. One catch: only one extension can control the browser proxy — disable other proxy/VPN extensions to avoid conflicts.
Is it open source?
Yes — MIT licensed, Manifest V3, vanilla JavaScript, no bundler. The shipped code matches the GitHub source almost verbatim. Read it on GitHub →
